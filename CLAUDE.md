# token-fantasy

"Token Fantasy", a Rust desktop app: this laptop's Claude Code and Codex sessions are the party of a 16-bit JRPG battle, the projects they work in (their git repositories) are the monsters. One process reads the sessions and plays the battle in a winit 0.30 + softbuffer 0.4 window (whole-number scaling, damage tracking). Linux only (procfs, kitty, Hyprland). Started 2026-10-28.

## Commands

- `cargo build --release`, then `target/release/token-fantasy` (live), `--demo` (a made-up battle), `--print` (sessions, tool calls, usage and quota as text), `--scale N` (initial size, default 3x of 426x266); `--mute`, `--unmute`, `--volume N`, `--pace wait|active` change the saved settings. Keys: 1-6 window size, f / F11 fullscreen, Esc close the card or config window / leave fullscreen, m mute, q quit.
- `cargo test`, `cargo clippy --all-targets` (kept warning-free), `cargo fmt` (`rustfmt.toml`: width 120).
- `cargo run --release --example preview -- <scenario> <prefix> <seconds>... [-- x y]`: scenarios (demo, full, ko, finish1, finish2, leave, limits1, limits2, mages, fighters) rendered to PNG without a window, with the battle log on stdout; `HOUR=22` for the night sky, `SCALE=n`, `-- x y` clicks at 1 s. The scene draws from its seed, so a scenario replays the same frames: compare before and after a scene change.
- After editing the art in `design/`: `node tools/gen_art.mjs` (writes `src/art.rs` and `src/art.bin`), then `cargo run --release --example screenshots` to refresh the README images in `docs/` (made-up sessions and projects; check the frames still catch the banners after a timing change).

## The rules (the user's)

- Each session is a player, each project a monster. At most 4 monsters and 8 players (2 columns of 4) on the field; sessions past the eighth wait off it behind a "+N WAIT" tab. The first session list sets the field at once (no banners, no run-ins); later newcomers run in, leavers run off.
- Claude Code sessions are mages (Sorcerer, Cleric, Spellsword, Summoner), Codex sessions fighters (Warrior, Thief, Monk, Lancer). A new session takes the least-used class of its group; a second player of a class wears its palette swap. Names come at random from the class's list in `design/art_players.js`, never twice on the field.
- A working session's time gauge fills in 8 s, then it attacks its monster; the damage is the tokens it burned since the last swing. Tokens arrive a few seconds behind the state (re-counted every 5 s), so a session that has just stopped swings what is pending at once. A benched session's tokens count, unseen.
- A tool call is a move, cosmetic only, at most one per player per 2.5 s: a mage casts Bash FIRE, Edit BLIZZARD, Read SCAN, Web GALE, TodoWrite PROTECT, anything else THUNDER, and its class special for a subagent (FLARE, HOLY, DOUBLECAST, SUMMON); a fighter answers every tool call with its special (CROSS CUT, STEAL, CHI BURST, JUMP). AskUserQuestion and ExitPlanMode make no move (the raised hand says it).
- A session waiting on the user raises a hand, "!" for a permission prompt, "?" for a question, with a chime.
- An idle session's HP is 999 x (1 - idle / 10 min). Its monster hits it about every 30 s (24-36), each hit taking it down to that line but never to 0; at 10 min the monster's finisher knocks it out (the field darkens, the monster's attack made big, a 2x red overkill number, a knock-back). A hit that lands after the session woke shows MISS. A session already idle when first seen starts with that HP. Under 25 % HP a player kneels.
- Any change to work cures a hurt player (CURE) and revives a knocked-out one (PHOENIX PLUME, with a chime); a change to waiting revives too.
- The 4 projects with the most sessions get monsters (a tie keeps the one already there, then the one seen first); a busier fifth makes the quietest monster flee. Sessions of a project without a monster fight the nearest one. A project's monster is picked by a hash of its path, unless another project has it out.
- When a project's last session ends, a player finishes its monster with its class's final blow (the session that ended, on its way out, if it was on the field; else the nearest player standing): an aura, the banner "<NAME> / FINAL BLOW", the move, a 2x number 3-4x the monster's biggest hit (at least 9999; cosmetic, not in the damage total), a flash and shock rings; then the monster is DEFEATED (banner with the project's damage, the victory fanfare) and dissolves. A doomed monster nobody finishes goes by itself after 10 s.
- The pace (config window, `--pace`): Wait spares sessions waiting on the user, Active lets monsters hit them too.
- The info window: GOLD is this month's list-price spend, EXP this month's tokens (Claude and Codex summed), MP the quota left (Claude 5 h and week, Codex week). The sky follows the clock: day 7-17 h, dusk 17-19 h, night.
- A click on a player or its party row opens its status card (2x portrait, job, Claude Code / Codex, project, state and for how long, HP, damage and on which monster, the session's spend and age, its last message in 4 lines of 64); its portrait brings the session's kitty window to the front when it shows the `>_` badge. A click on the card or off any player closes it. A click on the info window opens CONFIG (SCREEN fullscreen, SOUND mute, PACE, CLOSE); it takes every click while open and closes after 30 s without one.
- A project is a git repository, known by its remote (origin, else the first; `git@github.com:a/b.git` and `https://github.com/a/b` alike as `github.com/a/b`), so clones, worktrees and subfolders meet one monster; a directory outside a repository with a remote is its own project. The windows show the last part.
- Sessions run remotely (claude.ai or the phone, through a remote-control server) stay out of the battle.
- Names are the app's own: don't bring in names from existing games.

## Layout

- `design/`: `common.js` (RGB565 colours, palette-string sprites with an automatic 1 px outline, the 5x7 font), `art_players.js` (the 8 classes composed from parts per pose, name lists, palette swaps), `art_monsters.js` (8 monsters, 3 frames each, as plain rows), `engine.js` (the design's battle in JS): the source of every pixel. `tools/gen_art.mjs` turns them into `src/art.rs` (palettes, class and monster tables, icons, font; skipped by rustfmt) and `src/art.bin` (every image's palette indexes, included by `art.rs`). Both generated.
- `src/scene/` + `src/gfx.rs` + `src/layout.rs`: the battle on a 426x266 RGB565 canvas (`mod.rs` the rules and the cast, `fx.rs` the effects, `ui.rs` the field and windows). An action is a timeline: each frame it gets the time it had reached and the time it reaches now, and fires whatever falls in between.
- `src/session.rs`: what the collectors tell the scene (`Session`, `Tool`, `Usage`, `Mana`).
- `src/collect/`: the live sessions. Claude: `~/.claude/sessions/<pid>.json`, counted only while its `procStart` equals field 22 of `/proc/<pid>/stat`; busy/idle/waiting, `waitingFor` "permission prompt" -> "!", else "?"; its transcript gives the last thing said, the tool calls a waiting session waits on, and new tool calls. Codex: `codex` processes (not `exec`, `mcp`, `app-server` and other helpers) and the rollouts they hold open, threads living in the remote-control daemon matched to their TUI by cwd and originator, `~/.codex/state_5.sqlite` as a fallback; work/idle from the last `task_started` vs `task_complete`/`turn_aborted`; tool calls from its own and its sub-agents' rollouts (not the guardian's), the `exec` code-mode script as edit (apply_patch), web (web_search) or bash. Codex logs no approval waits. `text.rs` cleans markdown into the card's line of ASCII.
- `src/feed.rs`: `Session`s from what was found (project, ages, spend, burn: the tokens a session burned since the app started, or all of them if it started after), one move per session per poll (a subagent wins, else the latest), and the `--demo` director. `src/project.rs`: projects from `.git/config`, read directly, remembered 5 min per directory. `src/usage.rs`: this month's tokens and dollars at list price per transcript, cached by size and mtime. `src/quota.rs`: the MP bars from the usage endpoints the CLIs use, with their OAuth tokens passed to curl on stdin (never on a command line, never logged), fetched at most every 3 min, a 429 cooldown, waybar's `~/.cache/polybar/claude-usage.json` taken when fresher. `src/terminal.rs`: kitty's window from the session's environment, `kitten @ focus-window` then hyprctl.
- `src/sound.rs`: the sounds, synthesized, rendered to WAV under `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/token-fantasy-desktop/` and played with `pw-play` (else `paplay`, `aplay`). `src/settings.rs`: mute, volume and pace in `~/.config/token-fantasy-desktop/settings.json`.
- `src/main.rs`: CLI and the feeder threads (sessions every 1 s, usage every 5 s, quota every 30 s) sending `Update`s over a channel. `src/app.rs`: the window; it converts the RGB565 canvas to 0xRRGGBB for softbuffer.
- Not yet tried for real: bringing a terminal up from the card (`bring_up`), the sounds by ear.

## Conventions

- Every folder the app writes is named `token-fantasy-desktop` (`~/.cache/`, `~/.config/`, `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR/`). Apart from those it only reads; the quota is its only network access.
- Demo data, tests and screenshots use made-up project and session names; keep real ones out of the repo.
